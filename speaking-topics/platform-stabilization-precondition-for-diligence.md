# Speaking Topic: Speaking Topic: Platform Stabilization as a Precondition for Technical Due Diligence

> **Keynote Syllabus:** Executive & Board Briefing | **Speaker:** [Dr. Shashwat Bishwen](https://shashwat.si) | **Canonical Link:** [https://shashwat.si/speaking-topics/platform-stabilization-precondition-for-diligence/](https://shashwat.si/speaking-topics/platform-stabilization-precondition-for-diligence/)

## Keynote Overview
An unstable platform makes every diligence finding provisional, because the system under review will not hold still.

## Core Keynote Thesis & Field Notes

Diligence assumes the system being reviewed is in a representative, stable state. When a platform is actively destabilizing, under unresolved state-transition risk or undocumented database topology strain, every diligence finding is provisional, because the system itself is still changing underneath the review.

I argue stabilization should precede, not follow, a diligence process whenever active instability is already known. Otherwise the acquirer is diligencing a moving target and pricing certainty they do not actually have.

This is a sequencing argument, not a cost argument. Stabilization work done before diligence is cheaper than discovering the same instability after close, under a different name.

## Key Takeaways for Founders, CEOs & Boards

- Diligence findings on an actively unstable platform are provisional, not final.
- Stabilization should precede diligence whenever instability is already known going in.
- Stabilizing before close is consistently cheaper than discovering the same instability after close.

## Executive Audience Q&A

### Q: Doesn't this just delay the deal timeline?

It can, modestly, but it replaces a provisional finding with a real one, which is usually worth more to both sides than the delay costs.

## Keynote FAQs

### Q: When should stabilization happen relative to diligence?

Before, whenever active instability is already known. Diligencing a system that is still changing produces findings that will not hold once the system settles.
