# Speaking Topic: Speaking Topic: AI Ethics & the Future of Intelligence

> **Keynote Syllabus:** Executive & Board Briefing | **Speaker:** [Dr. Shashwat Bishwen](https://shashwat.si) | **Canonical Link:** [https://shashwat.si/speaking-topics/ai-ethics-future-of-intelligence/](https://shashwat.si/speaking-topics/ai-ethics-future-of-intelligence/)

## Keynote Overview
The Singularity is a spiritual mirror, and we are digitizing our own shadows faster than we are examining them.

## Core Keynote Thesis & Field Notes

The technological elite treats the Singularity as an engineering milestone: a coordinates shift on an exponential compute timeline. But technology only magnifies our internal state. If we digitize anxiety and greed, we build high-speed synthetic traps, not progress.

Life 3.0 asks a harder question than most AI keynotes attempt: as biological intelligence merges with computational systems, are we ready, not technically, but as people, for what that merger actually demands of us.

I bring this back to something concrete every time: the ethics of agentic AI are not abstract, they show up as a liability line item the first time an unsupervised agent does something a human would have known not to do.

## Key Takeaways for Founders, CEOs & Boards

- Technology magnifies existing human tendencies rather than correcting them by default.
- Readiness for AI-human merger is a human question first, a technical one second.
- AI ethics becomes concrete and auditable the moment agent autonomy is involved.

## Executive Audience Q&A

### Q: Is this talk pessimistic about AI?

No. It argues that the technology itself is neutral and simply magnifies whatever we already bring to it, which is actually a call to bring something worth magnifying.

### Q: How does this connect to your technical work?

Directly. The liability audits I run on agentic systems are this philosophy applied concretely: unexamined authority, at any scale, tends to cause harm.

## Keynote FAQs

### Q: What is the central argument in the "Singularity as a spiritual mirror" talk?

That technology magnifies whatever internal state we bring to it, so digitizing anxiety and greed at scale builds synthetic traps rather than progress, regardless of how advanced the underlying compute is.
