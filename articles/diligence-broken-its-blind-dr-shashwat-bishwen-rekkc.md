# Diligence Is Not Broken. It's Blind

> **Category:** Private Equity & Diligence | **Author:** [Dr. Shashwat Bishwen](https://shashwat.si) | **Canonical Link:** [https://shashwat.si/articles/diligence-broken-its-blind-dr-shashwat-bishwen-rekkc/](https://shashwat.si/articles/diligence-broken-its-blind-dr-shashwat-bishwen-rekkc/)

## Executive Summary
Standard technical diligence fails because it protects corporate assumptions instead of stress-testing operational realities. If diligence is conducted without inspecting runtime telemetry, it is legal reassurance, not engineering diligence.

## Deep Dive

The venture capital and private equity industries regularly express frustration over post-acquisition surprises: unbudgeted cloud infrastructure spending, fragile platforms that cannot scale, and engineering teams that cannot ship features.

They complain that technical diligence is broken.

Diligence is not broken. It is blind.

Traditional diligence is designed by lawyers and accountants who treat software as an intellectual property asset rather than a living, dynamic thermodynamic system.

They review contracts, verify open-source licenses, and confirm that unit test suites exist. They rarely inspect the runtime physics of the platform.

Diligence as a Mirror: If technical diligence does not stress-test database query execution under synthetic peak load, audit the deployment rollback ratio, and measure key-person dependency across tribal runbooks, it is not diligence. It is ceremonial legal reassurance.

Forensic diligence looks past the pitch deck to expose the hidden operational fractures before capital is deployed.

## Frequently Asked Architectural Questions (FAQs)

### Q: Why do standard technical due diligence processes miss real operational risk?

They are designed by lawyers and accountants who treat software as an intellectual property asset: reviewing contracts, licenses, and test coverage, rather than stress-testing the platform as the dynamic, load-bearing system it actually is.

### Q: What makes technical diligence genuinely forensic instead of ceremonial?

Stress-testing database query execution under synthetic peak load, auditing the deployment rollback ratio, and measuring key-person dependency across tribal runbooks, not just confirming a test suite and a SOC2 certificate exist.
