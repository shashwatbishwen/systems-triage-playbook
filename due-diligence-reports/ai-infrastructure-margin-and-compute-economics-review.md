# Due Diligence Report: Due Diligence Report: AI Infrastructure Margin & Compute Economics

> **Scope:** Margin review of committed AI infrastructure spend ahead of a product launch, anonymized. | **Risk Rating:** `Elevated` | **Author:** [Dr. Shashwat Bishwen](https://shashwat.si) | **Canonical Link:** [https://shashwat.si/due-diligence-reports/ai-infrastructure-margin-and-compute-economics-review/](https://shashwat.si/due-diligence-reports/ai-infrastructure-margin-and-compute-economics-review/)

## Executive Brief
Testing whether committed AI infrastructure capital is tied to a real margin model, or only to a capability narrative.

## Forensic Diligence Summary

This review tested whether capital committed to specialized AI infrastructure, including GPU clusters and custom compute, was tied to a margin model or justified primarily by a capability narrative.

## Audit Findings & Risk Remediation Matrix

### [ELEVATED RISK] Margin model completeness

**Observation:** No formal model existed linking infrastructure spend to the revenue or adoption threshold required to justify it; the investment case rested on capability description alone.

**Actionable Remediation:** Build an explicit margin model before further capital is committed, tying spend to a specific adoption threshold.

### [MODERATE RISK] Spend sequencing against adoption

**Observation:** Infrastructure procurement was running ahead of actual product adoption, rather than scaling with it.

**Actionable Remediation:** Resequence remaining spend to track realized adoption rather than projected adoption.

### [MODERATE RISK] Joint ownership of the decision

**Observation:** The infrastructure decision had been approved by technical leadership without a corresponding finance or margin sign-off.

**Actionable Remediation:** Require joint architecture and margin sign-off for future AI infrastructure commitments of this scale.

## Conclusion & Capital Recommendations

The infrastructure itself was technically sound. The risk was entirely in the absence of a margin model governing it, which this review converted from an unpriced bet into a tracked, threshold-based decision.

## Diligence & Risk Advisory FAQs

### Q: What is the biggest risk in AI infrastructure capital decisions?

Approving spend based on capability alone, without a margin model tying it to a specific adoption or revenue threshold that justifies the capital.
