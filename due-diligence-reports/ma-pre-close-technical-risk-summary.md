## Executive Brief
A consolidated technical risk summary built to be read by a deal committee, not just an engineering team.

## Forensic Diligence Summary

This summary consolidated findings from database, observability cost, key-person dependency, and disaster recovery assessments into a single technical risk view for the acquisition committee ahead of close.

## Audit Findings & Risk Remediation Matrix

### [ELEVATED RISK] Aggregate technical risk rating

**Observation:** No single finding across the underlying assessments was independently deal-breaking, but the combination of database scalability risk, key-person dependency, and single-provider resilience risk compounded into a materially elevated post-close integration risk.

**Actionable Remediation:** Price a defined technical remediation budget into the deal terms rather than treating these as post-close surprises.

### [MODERATE RISK] Remediation sequencing

**Observation:** Several findings had dependencies on each other; addressing database topology risk before key-person documentation risk would have been more expensive than the reverse order.

**Actionable Remediation:** Sequence remediation explicitly in the first 100 days post-close, in dependency order, not severity order alone.

### [ELEVATED RISK] Integration team continuity risk

**Observation:** The combination of undocumented tribal knowledge and a planned post-close engineering leadership transition created compounding continuity risk beyond either factor alone.

**Actionable Remediation:** Delay the planned leadership transition until the highest-priority documentation gaps are closed, or retain transitional advisory coverage through that window.

## Conclusion & Capital Recommendations

Individually moderate risks compounded into an elevated aggregate risk primarily through sequencing and timing, not technical severity alone. A deal committee reading only the individual reports would have under-priced the combined exposure.

## Diligence & Risk Advisory FAQs

### Q: Why summarize multiple technical findings into one report for a deal committee?

Individual findings can look moderate in isolation but compound into materially higher aggregate risk once sequencing, timing, and dependency between them are accounted for.
