# Due Diligence Report: Due Diligence Report: Pre-Investment Technical Validation Review

> **Scope:** Technical and product validation review ahead of a growth-stage capital commitment, anonymized. | **Risk Rating:** `Material` | **Author:** [Dr. Shashwat Bishwen](https://shashwat.si) | **Canonical Link:** [https://shashwat.si/due-diligence-reports/pre-investment-technical-validation-review/](https://shashwat.si/due-diligence-reports/pre-investment-technical-validation-review/)

## Executive Brief
Testing whether a founding team can name the cheapest experiment that would disprove its core assumption, before capital scales the bet.

## Forensic Diligence Summary

This review tested whether the team's foundational product assumption had been genuinely validated, or only supported by synthetic surveys and internal conviction, ahead of a capital commitment to scale it.

## Audit Findings & Risk Remediation Matrix

### [MATERIAL RISK] Disprovability of the core assumption

**Observation:** The team could not name, within a short window, the cheapest experiment that would conclusively disprove its foundational assumption, indicating the assumption had not actually been stress-tested.

**Actionable Remediation:** Design and run that experiment before further capital is committed to scaling the current model.

### [MODERATE RISK] Source of existing validation evidence

**Observation:** Prior validation relied primarily on synthetic surveys and stated intent rather than real behavioral signals such as payment or binding commitment.

**Actionable Remediation:** Replace survey-based validation with behavioral evidence, such as real transactions, before the next funding milestone.

### [MODERATE RISK] Decoupling of demand validation from build maturity

**Observation:** Engineering investment had outpaced demand validation, with production-grade infrastructure built before the core assumption was confirmed.

**Actionable Remediation:** Freeze further infrastructure investment until the core assumption passes a real behavioral test.

## Conclusion & Capital Recommendations

The team's engineering execution was strong. The capital risk was entirely upstream of the code, in an assumption that had never been placed under genuine pressure to fail.

## Diligence & Risk Advisory FAQs

### Q: What is the single test for whether a startup's core assumption has been validated?

Whether the team can name the cheapest experiment that would conclusively disprove it, and whether that experiment has actually been run, using real behavioral evidence rather than surveys.
