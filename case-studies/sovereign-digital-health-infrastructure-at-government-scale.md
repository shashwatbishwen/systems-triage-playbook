# Case Study: Case Study: Sovereign Digital Health Infrastructure at Government Scale

> **Client Profile:** A national-scale government digital health initiative spanning multiple ministries and public health programs. | **Author:** [Dr. Shashwat Bishwen](https://shashwat.si) | **Canonical Link:** [https://shashwat.si/case-studies/sovereign-digital-health-infrastructure-at-government-scale/](https://shashwat.si/case-studies/sovereign-digital-health-infrastructure-at-government-scale/)

## Problem Context
Building the core digital health data backbone across eighteen government ministries, grounded in rural fieldwork rather than boardroom assumption.

## The Problem Architecture

A national health data initiative needed a core digital backbone capable of serving a population-scale health mission, coordinated across eighteen separate government ministries with no shared technical standard between them.

## Technical Intervention & Approach

The architecture work was paired with firsthand ethnographic fieldwork across rural India, through Project Bharat By Bhartiya, rather than relying on policy assumptions about how the system would actually be used at the point of care.

## Verified Measurable Outcomes & ROI

The resulting architecture established the technical foundation for the national health mission, Ayushman Bharat, safeguarding coverage for over 10 million citizens across the coordinated ministries.

### Key Performance Proof

- **Government ministries coordinated:** `18`
- **Citizens safeguarded:** `10M+`

## Architectural Lessons & FAQs

### Q: Why pair government-scale architecture work with rural fieldwork?

A system meant to serve a population at the point of care has to be designed around how care actually happens on the ground, not only around ministry-level policy assumptions.
