# Case Study: Case Study: Enterprise Data Unification Across Legacy Silos

> **Client Profile:** A multi-division enterprise with data fragmented across dozens of legacy, siloed systems. | **Author:** [Dr. Shashwat Bishwen](https://shashwat.si) | **Canonical Link:** [https://shashwat.si/case-studies/enterprise-data-unification-across-legacy-silos/](https://shashwat.si/case-studies/enterprise-data-unification-across-legacy-silos/)

## Problem Context
Ninety-plus disconnected enterprise data sources, unified without a rip-and-replace program.

## The Problem Architecture

Invisible architectural drag was compounding across siloed systems, with no single source of truth for operational data. A full rip-and-replace modernization program had been proposed, priced, and shelved twice as too risky and too slow.

## Technical Intervention & Approach

Instead of replacing the legacy systems, autonomous, real-time ingestion topologies were built across the existing sources, eliminating systemic friction at the integration layer rather than at the source systems themselves.

## Verified Measurable Outcomes & ROI

Over 90 enterprise data sources were unified without a rewrite of the underlying legacy systems. Operating overhead across the data layer was collapsed by 36 percent, and ingestion velocity improved by 29 percent.

### Key Performance Proof

- **Data sources unified:** `90+`
- **Operating overhead reduction:** `36%`
- **Ingestion velocity improvement:** `29%`

## Architectural Lessons & FAQs

### Q: Is a full rewrite necessary to unify fragmented enterprise data?

Not always. Building autonomous, real-time ingestion topologies at the integration layer can unify dozens of legacy sources without replacing the underlying systems.
