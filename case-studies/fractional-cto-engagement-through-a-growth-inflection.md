# Case Study: Case Study: A Fractional CTO Engagement Through a Growth Inflection

> **Client Profile:** A venture-backed company's board, ahead of a major infrastructure and AI integration decision at a growth inflection point. | **Author:** [Dr. Shashwat Bishwen](https://shashwat.si) | **Canonical Link:** [https://shashwat.si/case-studies/fractional-cto-engagement-through-a-growth-inflection/](https://shashwat.si/case-studies/fractional-cto-engagement-through-a-growth-inflection/)

## Problem Context
A board brought in advisory before a hyper-scale infrastructure decision, not after it had already gone wrong.

## The Problem Architecture

The company was approaching a hyper-scale infrastructure decision, including early agentic AI integration, with internal technical leadership under pressure to move fast and no outside technical voice at the board level to pressure-test the plan.

## Technical Intervention & Approach

A fractional CTO advisory engagement was structured specifically around major decision points rather than day-to-day operations, acting as a strategic sparring partner for the founders and board before capital was committed to the infrastructure plan.

## Verified Measurable Outcomes & ROI

The velocity pressure the team was under was explicitly priced against a technical debt covenant before the infrastructure build began, rather than discovered as unpriced borrowing months later. The board retained an outside technical check at exactly the decision point where it mattered.

### Key Performance Proof

- **Engagement timing:** `Before infrastructure commitment, not after`
- **Primary deliverable:** `Priced technical debt covenant`

## Architectural Lessons & FAQs

### Q: When is the highest-value point to bring in fractional CTO advisory?

Before a major infrastructure or AI integration decision is finalized, when outside technical judgment can still change the outcome rather than audit it after the fact.
